Kakobuy spreadsheet guide

Use Kakobuysheets as a route map: pick a category, open the live spreadsheet, compare sellers, then let warehouse QC decide what ships.

1. Start with a category

Jump into Shoes, Hoodies, T-Shirts, Jackets, Pants, Headwear, Accessories, or Other instead of scrolling a raw dump.

2. Treat rows as discovery

Spreadsheet prices, seller photos, and notes help you shortlist. They are not a final quality promise.

3. Review QC before shipping

After the item arrives at Kakobuy, check shape, sizing, labels, materials, hardware, print placement, and visible defects. That warehouse layer is the real decision point.

4. Order on Kakobuy

Payments, warehouse storage, consolidation, and international shipping stay on Kakobuy. This site only helps you find and open listings.
